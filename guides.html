IMPORTANT

ROLEPLAY RULES
& NRP GUIDE

Good Roleplay keeps the city alive. These rules protect the experience for everyone — break them and staff can take action.

What is Non-Roleplay (NRP)?

Non-Roleplay means actions that are unrealistic, break immersion, or create poor-quality scenes. If it would never make sense in a real city story — it’s probably NRP. Below are the most common examples on Global Era RP.

01

Cop Baiting NRP

Provoking police or emergency services for no realistic reason. Don’t force scenes just for chaos.

02

Soldier Baiting NRP

Provoking Army faction members without a proper story reason. Military is not your toy.

03

Base Gate Camping

You can’t just gather near military base gates. Only allowed during official events.

04

Weapons Near Cops / Soldiers

Don’t pull weapons in front of police or soldiers. They can arrest you — or open fire.

05

Open Carry in the City

Openly carrying weapons in the city is forbidden. Keep it realistic and hidden unless the scene allows it.

06

Attacking Cops Without Reason

Don’t attack a police officer when you are not wanted and they are not trying to arrest you.

07

Hostage Rules

Taking cops or soldiers as hostages is not allowed. Choose realistic targets for serious scenes.

08

Mercy Killing

Asking a friend to kill you so you can “reset” is NRP. Play out your injuries and consequences.

09

Unrealistic Stunts & Ramming

No movie stunts or using expensive vehicles just to ram others. Drive like a person who values their life and car.

10

Vehicle Spawning Abuse

No spawning vehicles into water or abusing spawn systems to gain unfair advantage.

11

Unrealistic Swimming

Don’t swim forever during a chase with no destination. Have a goal — or get out of the water.

12

Disconnecting Mid-RP

If you disconnect during a scene, reconnect and inform the other players. Provide proof if you can’t return.

13

Ignoring RP Commands

Answer roleplay commands directed at you (example: /do). Ignoring them kills the scene.

14

Going Out of Character

Repeatedly breaking character during roleplay ruins immersion for everyone. Stay in character.

15

New Life Rule (NLR) STRICT

After death, forget everything from the last 15 minutes. Do not return to the same place to hunt the players who killed you.

16

Hospital Camping

Do not wait near the hospital to kill or damage players who just respawned. Give them a chance to recover.

17

Respect Law Enforcement

Act like you fear and respect the police. You should not want to get caught — play smart, not suicidal.

18

Reckless Driving

Constant high speed, crashing into everything, and treating the city like a demolition derby is NRP.

19

Hit & Run Reality

If you crash, react to it. Driving away like nothing happened breaks the story of the world.

Pro Tip — Play the Story, Not the Kill

The best scenes come from fear, negotiation, and consequences — not from who shoots first. Treat every character (including your own) like a real person who wants to survive and build something in the city. When in doubt: slow down, talk, and roleplay the outcome.
